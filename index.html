    Questions

    Things people often wonder.

    Is it confidential?

    Yes. What you share stays between us, except where safety law requires otherwise. I'll explain this in your first session.

    Online or in person?

    Both. Choose whichever feels easier for you.

    How many sessions will I need?

    It varies. We review together every few sessions.

    What if I don't have a 'disorder'?

    You don't need one. Many people come for stress, decisions, relationships or growth.

    What does it cost?

    See fees.

    Are the tools here therapy?

    No. They're self-care practices. Therapy is a relationship with a trained professional.
